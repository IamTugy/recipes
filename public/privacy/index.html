Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Tugy's Cookbook ("the app") is a personal recipe collection app. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and how it's handled. If anything here is unclear, contact tugmica@gmail.com.

Account information

Signing in is handled by Clerk, a third-party authentication provider. Clerk may collect your email address, name, and profile photo depending on how you sign in (email, Google, etc). See Clerk's privacy policy for details on how they handle this data. The app itself stores your Clerk user ID to associate recipes, favorites, and ratings with your account.

Content you provide

  • Recipes you create, edit, or import (titles, ingredients, steps, photos, notes)
  • Photos you upload, including AI-enhanced versions of your photos
  • Ratings, favorites, and shopping list items
  • Feature requests or feedback you submit
  • Text, URLs, or photos you submit for AI-assisted recipe import

Uploaded photos are stored on Cloudflare R2 object storage. Recipe data is stored in a MongoDB database operated by the app.

AI features

Features like AI recipe import, photo enhancement, translation, and nutrition estimates send the relevant text or image data to Google's Gemini API for processing. This data is sent solely to generate the requested result and is not used by this app for any other purpose. Refer to Google's Gemini API terms for how Google handles data sent to their API.

What this app does not do

  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No selling or sharing your data with third parties for marketing
  • No analytics/tracking SDKs beyond basic, self-hosted view/rating counters on recipes

Data retention & deletion

Your recipes, favorites, and account data are retained as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email tugmica@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy: tugmica@gmail.com.
